🚀 47kg to 55kg Plan

The 10-to-7 Workday Schedule

  • 8:30 AM: Kettle Oats (50g) + Hot Water + PB + 1 Banana.
  • 11:30 AM: 2 Boiled Eggs + 1 Banana.
  • 1:30 PM: Heavy Mess Lunch.
  • 6:30 PM: Sand-Roasted Peanuts (Commute Snack).
  • 8:30 PM: Hostel Dinner + Milky Mist Plain Curd.
  • 9:45 PM: 40g Soya Chunks (Soaked in Kettle).

⚡ Quick Surplus Boosters

  • Banana PB Oat Shake (Breakfast/Weekends): Blend 2 bananas, 2 spoons peanut butter, 30g oats, and milk. (~600 cals, 15g protein). Easy, heavy liquid calories.
  • Dates & Almonds (Office Desk): Keep a small box at work. Eat 3-4 dates + 10 almonds for a mid-day energy spike. (~150 cals, instant carbs & healthy fats).
  • Avocado / Sharjah Shake (Evening Outings): When in Ernakulam, order these instead of fresh juice. (~350-400 cals, 8-10g protein).
  • Roasted Chana (Study/Movie Snack): A great alternative to peanuts if you want high protein but lower fat.
  • Extra Boiled Eggs (Anytime): The ultimate protein cheat code. Boil 1 or 2 extra in the kettle if you are short on your 90g daily goal.

🌯 Outside / Treat Meals (Bulking Edition)

  • Shawarma Roll: Excellent fast food choice. Loaded with chicken and carbs. Ask for less mayo if your stomach is sensitive. (~400 cals, 15-20g pro). Best for evening outings.
  • Alfaham / Tandoori Chicken (Quarter): Pure protein goldmine. Pair it with 1-2 kuboos for clean carbs instead of oily parottas. (~350 cals, 30g pro).
  • Beef Roast & Porotta: Massive calorie bomb. Great for hitting your surplus, but limit to 1-2 times a week so the heavy oils don't stall your digestion overnight. (~600+ cals, 25g pro).

Target

Hit 2400 Calories & 90g Protein daily to gain ~2.3kg/month.
