🚀 47kg to 55kg Master Plan

1. The 10-to-7 Workday Schedule

  • 8:30 AM (Wake Up): Kettle Oats (50g) + Hot Water + 1-2 Spoons Peanut Butter + 1 Banana. (Clean energy).
  • 11:30 AM (Office Desk): 2 Boiled Eggs (boiled in morning) + 1 Banana.
  • 1:30 PM (Lunch): Heavy Rice/Roti with Dal or Chicken/Fish. Eat until full.
  • 6:30 PM (Commute): 1 Handful (30g) of Sand-Roasted Peanuts. (Prevents hunger crash in traffic).
  • 8:30 PM (Dinner): Hostel Dinner + 1 small ₹12 Milky Mist Plain Curd. (Aids digestion).
  • 9:45 PM (Pre-Bed): 40g Soya Chunks soaked in kettle water for 15 mins. (The ultimate protein cheat code).

2. The "No-Gas" Gut Rules

  • Avoid: Deep-fried bakery items (samosas, puffs), heavy ghee/oils before bed, and flavored yogurts with added sugar.
  • Do: Drink warm water from the kettle after heavy meals. Shower before you eat, not after. Eat curd daily to maintain probiotics.

3. The Targets

To gain ~2.3 kg per month, you must hit: 2400 Calories and 90g Protein every single day.
